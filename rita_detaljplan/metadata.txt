about=Draw detailed development plans (detaljplaner) in QGIS following the Swedish Land Survey's national information specification for detailed development plans (v4.1) and Boverket's plan provision catalogue. Checks the plan against the specification's rules, delivers it to the National Geodata Platform (NGP) or saves a delivery file, creates the plan's legend in the print layout, and tags the plan description (Word) digitally according to Boverket's BFS 2020:8, linking the motives to the plan's provisions. Plans can be stored in GeoPackage or PostGIS, with check-out and check-in for fast editing. The user interface and documentation are in Swedish. Website: https://ritaplan.xyz. Early development version.
